Nutrition Tips for Indian Cuisine

Make the most of your meal plans with these expert tips on Indian cooking.

Spices as Medicine

Many Indian spices like turmeric, cumin, and coriander have medicinal properties. Turmeric contains curcumin, which has powerful anti-inflammatory effects and is a strong antioxidant.

Try to incorporate a variety of spices in your daily cooking for both flavor and health benefits.

Mindful Oil Usage

Traditional Indian cooking can be oil-heavy. Opt for healthier oils like mustard oil or cold-pressed groundnut oil in moderation.

Consider using an air fryer or non-stick cookware to reduce oil consumption without compromising on taste.

Balance Your Thali

A traditional Indian thali (plate) naturally incorporates all food groups. Aim for a balanced plate with roti/rice, dal, vegetables, curd, and a small amount of pickle or chutney.

This natural balance ensures you get a good mix of carbs, proteins, fats, and micronutrients.
